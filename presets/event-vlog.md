# Preset: event / vlog / family / behind the scenes

Use for parties, weddings (highlight cut), conferences, open houses, daily vlogs and team events.

## Structure
- Mostly chronological (use clip creation time and file order).
- Opening: an energetic or emotional moment, or a strong establishing shot.
- Middle: alternate wide shots (where are we) with close shots (faces, details, reactions).
- Ending: a calm or emotional moment, group shot, or the venue leaving / sunset.

## Shots
- 1.5-3 s for energetic sections, 3-5 s for emotional moments.
- Faces and reactions beat empty rooms. Prefer shots where people are looking up and smiling.
- Keep short speech moments (toast lines, a laugh) whole; check `speech_sec` in the analysis.
- Avoid more than 2 shots in a row from the same angle.

## Sound
Mixed: keep sound on speech and key moments; offer "keep only on speech clips" as the recommended audio choice.

## Default length
60-180 s landscape, 30-60 s portrait.
