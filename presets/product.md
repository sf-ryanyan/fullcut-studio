# Preset: product / store / food / service showcase

## Structure
- Opening (first 2 s): the hero shot - the product at its best, or the finished result.
- Then: details (materials, texture, features) -> product in use / process -> result -> closing hero or logo shot from the footage.
- For "before / after" work (cleaning, repair, renovation): before -> process -> after; keep the same angle for before and after if possible.

## Shots
- 1.5-3 s each; slow detail glides up to 4 s.
- Sharp focus is critical - drop any shot where the product is soft.
- No people's faces cut in half; hands-in-frame shots are good for "in use".

## Sound
Usually silent (ready for music or voice-over); keep sound for satisfying ASMR moments if asked.

## Default length
15-45 s for social, 60 s for website.
