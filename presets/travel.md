# Preset: travel / landscape / city

## Structure
- Opening: the most stunning wide or aerial shot.
- Group by place, then by time of day (morning -> night). Use creation time to keep the story honest.
- Rhythm: wide -> medium -> detail for each place, then move on.
- Ending: sunset, night lights, or a slow wide shot.

## Shots
- Landscapes and drone shots 4-6 s; street/detail shots 1.5-3 s.
- Use complete slow moves; avoid shaky walking shots unless they are the only record of a place.
- Prefer golden-hour and well-exposed clips; drop clips facing straight into the sun.

## Sound
Usually silent output (wind noise) ready for music; keep sound for strong ambience (waves, markets) if the user wants.

## Default length
60-120 s landscape, 30-45 s portrait.
